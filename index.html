Construction Quality

Built With Care. Crafted With Quality.

A beautiful lifestyle needs a strong foundation. Shivam Residency is created with attention to construction quality, materials, and long-term comfort.

Structure

  • RCC framed structure confirming relevant BIS codes & specifications.
  • RCC design will be earthquake resistant to prescribed richter scale.
  • Testing of Casting Cubes will be done.
  • ISI mark steel will be used.
  • Quality AAC blocks of BIS standard for brick work.
  • Sand plaster internal walls.
  • Gypsum plaster for internal walls facing the exterior side.

Flooring

  • Vitrified flooring of 1200 mm × 800 mm in flats.
  • Anti-skid tiles in the terrace and dry balcony.
  • Full height designer tiles in toilets with anti-skid ceramic flooring.

Doors & Windows

  • Attractive main entrance door with solid wood.
  • Godrej / Europa mortise lock.
  • Both sides have laminated doors for bedrooms.
  • Flush doors for bathrooms.
  • Granite window sill.
  • Aluminium windows with premium quality mosquito net with MS safety grill.

Kitchen

  • Granite kitchen platform with stainless steel sink.
  • Provision for water purifier.
  • Designer tiles up to ceiling above kitchen platform.
  • Exhaust fan in kitchen.

Electrical

  • Concealed fire retardant wires of Polycab / Anchor.
  • Premium modular switches.
  • Provision for inverter.
  • Provision for AC point.
  • Provision for internet cable point.

Plumbing

  • Solar water heater.
  • Concealed plumbing with premium quality CPVC pipes.
  • Sanitary ware of Jaguar / Hindware / Cera.
  • Exhaust fan provision in bathrooms.
  • Exclusive range of CP fittings.
